Got a layout, or feature set in mind that doesn't exist yet? Maybe you want to add a feature or two to an already popular open source build? I build fully custom boards from scratch, scoped around what you actually need.

How it works

  • Process varies — sometimes I design and check in at key points, sometimes it's a full back-and-forth, sometimes you hand me a wishlist and I build to it
  • For more specific requests, expect a collaborative process — you'll weigh in on layout, wireless vs wired, split vs unibody, and everything else
  • Typical cost runs $200–$400+ depending on switches, wireless module, case material, and other options
  • Timeline is usually 1–2 months, longer depending on parts availability and complexity — rush option available

  • Previous builds available to view before you commit, just ask
